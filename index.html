🚀 Getting Started

Follow these simple steps to get the app running on your Mac in under two minutes.

  1. Visit the download page: Click the big button above or go to github.com/…/releases.
  2. Download the app: Look for the latest release and download the .zip file named something like zielzeit-scalable-capital.zip.
  3. Extract the archive: Double-click the downloaded .zip file. macOS will extract it and show you the application file (usually named zielzeit-scalable-capital.app).
  4. Move to Applications folder (optional): Drag the app into your Applications folder for easy access. You can also run it directly from the Downloads folder.
  5. Open the app: Double-click the app. The first time you may see a warning that the app is from an unidentified developer. Right-click (or Control-click) the app and select "Open" to bypass this.
  6. Enjoy in your menu bar: The app will appear as a small icon in your macOS menu bar (top right). Click it to see your portfolio projection.

📋 What this app does

This is a read-only menu bar application. It connects to your Scalable Capital account via the official Scalable CLI and shows you:

  • Your current portfolio value
  • A projected date when your portfolio will reach your savings goal
  • Simple charts powered by Swift Charts to visualize your progress

No transactions, no buy/sell buttons — just a clean, at-a-glance tracker for your FIRE journey.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. The app does not store or transmit your login credentials. It uses the official Scalable CLI which handles authentication securely. The app only reads your portfolio data locally.

Do I need to keep the CLI running?

No. The app calls the CLI in the background when you click the menu bar icon. Make sure the CLI is installed and you have authenticated once.

Can I set any goal amount?

Yes. You can set your target portfolio value directly in the app's preferences (click the menu bar icon, then "Settings").

Why is the app unsigned?

This is a free, open-source project. The developer has not purchased an Apple Developer certificate. Your Mac may show a warning — just right-click and open as described above.

💡 Tips for using the app

  • Keep the app running in the background — it will update your projection every time you click the menu bar icon.
  • Adjust your goal amount to see different projection dates. Play with the numbers to stay motivated.
  • Use the built-in charts to track your growth over weeks and months.
  • The app is deliberately minimal. No notifications, no distractions — just the information you need.
